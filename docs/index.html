Why MiniEQ

Just the knobs you actually reach for.

Big equalizer apps bury the basics under presets and subscriptions. MiniEQ is five knobs and nothing else.

Every app at once

Music, Spotify, YouTube, movies and games all go through the same five knobs.

Lives in the menu bar

One click opens the panel. There's no Dock icon and no window to keep track of.

No drivers, no password

Built on Apple's own system-audio capture in macOS 14.4 and later. Nothing gets installed into the system.

Volume where macOS has none

Many HDMI and DisplayPort monitors ignore the volume keys. MiniEQ's Volume knob works on every output.

Follows your output

Plug in headphones, connect AirPods or switch speakers, and MiniEQ moves with you automatically.

Quit means untouched

Quit MiniEQ and your Mac's sound goes straight back to normal. Nothing is ever recorded or saved.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

MiniEQ needs macOS 14.4 (Sonoma) or later and runs on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Download

Get MiniEQ-1.0.dmg and open it.

Drag to Applications

Drag MiniEQ onto the Applications shortcut in the window, then open it from your Applications folder.

Approve it once

If macOS blocks the first launch, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. When MiniEQ asks for system audio, click Allow.

macOS blocks the first launch because MiniEQ isn't notarized by Apple. MiniEQ asks to “record” system audio because that's how it passes your sound through the knobs — the audio never leaves your Mac and nothing is saved.

How it works

Real-time tone controls, done the macOS way.

MiniEQ uses the system-audio tap Apple added in macOS 14.4, so it needs no audio driver or kernel extension.

Build it yourself

One command from source.

MiniEQ is about a thousand lines of Swift with no dependencies. All you need is Apple's command-line tools (xcode-select --install).

$ git clone https://github.com/thisFunction/MiniEq.git
$ cd MiniEq
$ ./build.sh install

Run ./build.sh dmg to make your own shareable disk image.

FAQ

Good to know.

Why does macOS say it can't verify MiniEQ?

MiniEQ isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to the message about MiniEQ. You only have to do this once.

Why does it ask to record system audio?

That's the permission macOS uses for any app that processes the sound other apps play. MiniEQ runs your audio through its knobs and straight back out to your speakers. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Does it work with AirPods, USB speakers and HDMI monitors?

Yes. MiniEQ uses whatever output is selected in Sound settings and follows you when you switch. Its Volume knob also works on monitors and TVs where the Mac's volume keys don't.

Can I use it alongside eqMac or another equalizer?

It's best to quit other equalizer apps while MiniEQ is running, so the effects don't stack on top of each other.

People on my video call hear an echo.

Echo cancellation in some calling apps can't account for re-routed audio. Quit MiniEQ for the call and open it again afterward.

How do I turn it off or uninstall it?

Click the knob icon in the menu bar and choose Quit; your sound returns to normal immediately. To uninstall, quit MiniEQ and drag it from Applications to the Trash. To start it automatically, add it in System Settings → General → Login Items.
