MiniEQ — Volume, Balance, Bass, Mid and Treble for everything your Mac plays.
Needs macOS 14.4 or later.

INSTALL
1. Drag MiniEQ onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open MiniEQ from your Applications folder. A small knob icon appears in the menu bar.

IF YOUR MAC SAYS IT CAN'T VERIFY MINIEQ
MiniEQ isn't notarized by Apple, so the first launch is blocked. To open it anyway:
1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
2. Scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to the message about MiniEQ.
3. Confirm with your password or Touch ID. You only have to do this once.

ALLOW SYSTEM AUDIO
MiniEQ asks to record system audio. Click Allow. It needs this to pass your sound
through its knobs. Nothing is recorded or saved.

USING IT
- Drag a knob up or down (hold Option for fine moves), or scroll over it.
- Double-click a knob to reset it.
- Quit other equalizer apps (like eqMac) while using MiniEQ.
- To turn MiniEQ off, click its menu bar icon and choose Quit. Your sound returns to normal right away.
